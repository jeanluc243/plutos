# Prompts exacts — Plutos

Outil : imagegen intégré. Une génération par variante.

## 1. 01-destination

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Create a refined original logo concept for PLUTOS, a China–Africa shipping, purchasing and order management brand. This is a reference-guided transformation into a NEW Plutos brand, not a reproduction of the reference company. Reference image 1 is ONLY a typography reference: match the visual DNA of the lowercase "ane" letters as closely as possible — very bold compact extended squared geometric sans, softened square corners, thick uniform strokes, tight custom kerning, solid industrial confidence. Apply that custom lettering language to exactly "plutos" in lowercase, p-l-u-t-o-s, all six letters fully readable. Reference image 2 is ONLY inspiration for minimal forward motion and negative space; reference image 3 is ONLY inspiration for a simple folded-paper motion symbol. Do not reproduce their logos or any of their contact details.
There must be a single Chinese character "达" (Unicode U+8FBE, simplified Chinese, pronounced dá), positioned to the LEFT of "plutos". Render 达 accurately and boldly, preserving its 大 and 辶 components, never fake Chinese glyphs. Character and word form one balanced horizontal brand lockup; "plutos" must remain prominent and easy to read, not tiny. The Chinese character acts as the standalone brand symbol. Harmonize its heavy geometric stroke styling with the wordmark.
Palette: cobalt blue #2455F5, deep navy #101A34, restrained amber #FFB547. Flat solid fills, razor sharp clean edges and vector-like precision; white #FFFFFF background. No mockup, no surface texture, no photographic lighting, no gradient, no shadows, no bevel, no watermark, no slogan, no TM. High-end identity design, strong small-size readability.
Canvas: landscape 1536x1024. One main logo only, horizontally centered with generous whitespace. Logo fills approximately 75% of canvas width and sits near the vertical center. Only tiny unobtrusive concept number in muted blue-gray at top left as specified below. No presentation ornaments, no decorative palette circles, no extra labels or words.
Concept number text: "01".
Direction: DESTINATION. Most faithful typographic approach to ANE. Unboxed cobalt 达 on the left; dark navy plutos immediately right; a small bold amber arrowhead accent pointing northeast at the upper-right end of the final s, deliberately distinct from ANE's arrow. Compact strong horizontal composition. 达 height about 110% of Latin ascender height. Beautiful custom squared lowercase letterforms, broad and bold, especially squared p bowl, squared u, rectangular rounded o. Keep the amber accent subtle.
```
